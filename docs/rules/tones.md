# Heist and Noir tones (Expanded Player's Guide, p.110–113)

This summary paraphrases the rules. Page numbers are EPG pages, and the full text is in `content/epg/tones.edn`. Both tones suit Manifest Destiny's
corporate-noir Android setting.

## Heist (p.110–111)
A job runs in four phases: planning the job, doing the legwork, pulling the trigger, and getting away with it.

**Heat** (new rule):
- **Gaining heat:** a Despair rolled during planning or legwork can be spent to give that character 1 heat.
- **Getting burned:** when a character with heat makes a check, the GM can spend their heat to upgrade its difficulty, 1 heat per
  upgrade and at most 3 upgrades per check.
- **Attention:** the GM can also spend heat to bring attention, in proportion to what the character did:
  - 1 heat: a patrol car drives by, or a flagged card.
  - 3 heat: an active search, or frozen accounts.
  - 5 heat: the feds get involved, a corporate assassin team is on the character's trail, or their face is all over the news.
- **Cooling off:** heat stays until the GM spends it or the character works it off in the story, for example with a favor for a crooked judge.
- **Limits:** heat is background attention. Open crimes, such as a shootout with the NAPD, bring their own trouble whatever the heat level.

## Noir (p.111–113)
**Internal Monologue** (new rule): once per encounter, a player can narrate their character's inner thoughts, plan
and reasoning to the group. The character heals up to 3 strain.

## In the app (later)
- Heat is a per-character counter on the sheet and the GM dashboard. When the GM spends Despair, they can choose "give heat".
- Internal Monologue goes on the sheet as a once-per-encounter button that heals 3 strain and logs it to the history.
- It's a campaign choice whether these interact with the Secrets and Obligations house rule. For example, heat could raise magnitude.
