## Secrets and Obligations
- **Replaces or changes:** Debt and Favors from Shadow of the Beanstalk. Neither is used at this table.
- **Source:** the table (Obligation-style).
- **Rule:** at character creation, each player picks or rolls one Secret or Obligation from the table below.
  - An **Obligation** is something the character owes or is bound to.
  - A **Secret** is something the character hides, and exposing it is the threat.
  - Both work the same way at the table. The GM ties each one to the campaign.

| d100 | Type | Kind | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01–10 | Blackmail | Secret | Someone has proof of what you did and wants something for it. |
| 11–20 | Debt | Obligation | You owe a corp or a person, and they will collect. |
| 21–30 | Family Duty | Obligation | Kin depend on you, or expect you to take a role you never chose. |
| 31–40 | Forbidden Knowledge | Secret | You know something a corp would kill to keep buried. |
| 41–50 | Bounty | Obligation | There's a price on you, legal or otherwise. |
| 51–60 | Oath | Obligation | You swore to see something through. |
| 61–70 | Addiction | Secret | A habit you hide, and a supplier who knows it. |
| 71–80 | Betrayal | Secret | You sold someone out, and they may be alive. |
| 81–90 | Criminal Record | Secret | A name, a face, a record you've buried. |
| 91–00 | Corporate Contract | Obligation | Your body, skills or future belong to a corp on paper. |

The first four types are the ones you named. The other six are my suggestions, so swap them freely.

**Magnitude (settled):** at creation, the player chooses a magnitude of **5, 10 or 15**. They gain that much starting XP: +5, +10 or +15. A bigger magnitude means a heavier burden and a bigger XP head start.

**Personal attachment (settled; accepted as drafted):** the inspiration is the r/genesysrpg thread
["Curses and Obligation in Genesys"](https://www.reddit.com/r/genesysrpg/comments/7sw5kj/curses_and_obligation_in_genesys/).
The thread couldn't be fetched from the tooling, so this draft follows the classic Obligation idea and hasn't been checked against the thread.
- Every Secret or Obligation names an **attachment**: the person, corp or thing it binds the character to, such as a blackmailer,
  a creditor corp or a sick sister. It also gets one sentence on **why it matters** to the character. The GM uses both.
- **Session check:** at the start of each session, the GM rolls d100 against the party's combined magnitudes, with each character holding a
  slice of that range.
  - If the roll lands in a character's slice, their Secret or Obligation **comes due**: the attachment shows up in the story that session.
  - The character whose slice was hit gets −2 strain threshold for the session, and everyone else gets −1.
  - On a roll of doubles, the effect doubles.
- **Changing magnitude in play:** dealing with it in the story (paying a debt down, ending a blackmail) lowers the magnitude.
  Ignoring it, or making it worse, raises the magnitude. Magnitude can also be taken on mid-campaign in exchange for help.

The table accepted these rules as drafted. Change them here if play shows they need it.
